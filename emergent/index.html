What the textbook hides

Electron and hole densities on a log scale. The shaded box is the textbook's sharp depletion region; the real carriers fade out over a few Debye lengths and never quite reach zero.

The equations being solved

Adaptive mesh with spacing ≈ ¼ Debye length; each Newton step solves a tridiagonal system with the Thomas algorithm in O(N); log damping keeps the exponentials stable. Quasi-Fermi levels: φp = 0, φn = VR.
