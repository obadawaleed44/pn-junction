How it works

The idea
The depletion region is a capacitor whose plates are W apart: C = εsA/W. Because W grows like √(Vbi + VR), 1/C² plotted against VR is a straight line.
Read the line
Where it crosses zero (at VR = −Vbi) gives the built-in voltage; its slope gives the doping N of the lightly doped side.
Sweep
Hands (pull apart), ← → keys (Shift = faster), or drag a lead outwards. A point is recorded every 0.2 V.
Check
Pick the diode that matches your fit, then open the package to see the junction you measured.
