How to use it

Hands
Turn on the camera (top right). Left hand is the − terminal, right hand the +. Pull apart to raise the reverse voltage.
Lock
Make a fist to lock the voltage, then lower your arms. Show both open palms to unlock.
No camera
Drag a metal contact outwards, pinch with two fingers, or use the arrow keys.
Turn the model
Drag empty space to rotate, scroll to zoom. The reset button (or R) brings the view back.
What is this?
Click any part of the model (a hole, an ion, the gap, a graph) for an explanation with live numbers. Clicking inside the gap also drops a + test charge.
Parameters
Open Physics (or G) to change material, temperature and doping, type an exact VR, and read the band diagram, ρ(x), E(x) and V(x).
